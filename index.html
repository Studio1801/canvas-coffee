          05 / Art house

          Stay for
          the art.

          Canvas exists to give makers of all kinds a place to show and share their work. The walls change with local and regional artists, and every bag of coffee carries one of Genavieve's paintings.

          Call for artists, spring 2027

          The Weekend
          Collection.

          A one-weekend curated exhibition and art sale, not a booth-style market. Enough room for each artist that visitors can step into their world and leave as collectors.

          10

          artists, at most

          8 to 10

          works each, 2-D

          60%

          of net sales to the artist

          Curated by Genavieve Gilbert

          What we look for

          • Artistic strength, originality and a distinct voice
          • A cohesive body of original work, with a story behind it
          • Professional presentation, ready to hang
          • How the collection fits the whole show: medium, scale, subject and price

          Following, résumé length and gallery representation do not decide acceptance. Artists at any career stage may apply. No application or participation fee.

          What Canvas provides

          • Curation, installation and the whole presentation
          • Promotion through social media and email, plus artist storytelling in the room
          • A collector-focused First Friday opening
          • Checkout, sales tax, an itemised statement, and payment within 10 business days

            07 / Wholesale

            Dialed in
            at your bar.

            We roast every wholesale coffee ourselves, here in Greenville. It is the same coffee we pour at the café, and we work with your team to get it right on your equipment, so it tastes good on a busy morning, not just at the tasting.

            What we roast for you

            • House and espresso blends, including a custom blend built around your menu
            • Single origins, with Genavieve's artwork on the label
            • Decaf, and quarterly limited releases
            • Private label, with your name on the bag

            Coworking spaces, churches, bookshops, restaurants and cafés across the Upstate. Some pour a few pounds a week, others around twenty-five.

            How it works

            • Send a few details. We reply within two business days.
            • Tasting: your menu, your equipment, your volume.
            • We set your pricing and open your account.
            • Reorder online, any time.

                Terms

                Last updated November 2026

                Café orders

                Café pickup orders and café gift cards are handled on Toast. Prices are before tax and may change.

                Coffee shop orders

                Bags are roasted in small batches. Orders are ready for pickup, delivered free within 5 miles, or shipped. Every Friday, every bag is 20% off.

                Subscriptions

                Every 2, 4 or 6 weeks, same price per bag. Cancel any time, no fees. The NEXT100 half-off first bag applies once per household; your price stays locked while you stay subscribed.

                Art, workshops and The Weekend Collection

                Work on the walls is sold on behalf of the artist. Weekend Collection artists receive 60% of net sale proceeds. Workshop seats are confirmed by email.
