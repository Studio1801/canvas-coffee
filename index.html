          Terms

          Last updated November 2026

          Ordering ahead

          Orders are made for the pickup time you choose. If the case sells out after you order, we will offer a swap or a refund at the counter. Orders not collected within an hour may be remade at our discretion.

          The Friday Takeaway

          Every Friday, every bag of coffee is 20% off in the shop and online. If a coffee is not on the shelf, order it online at the same price and pick it up once it is roasted.

          Subscriptions

          Subscriptions renew every two, four or six weeks. Skip, pause or cancel any time before the next roast day. The half-off first bag applies once per household while the first 100 spots last.

          Art, prints and reservations

          Work on the walls is sold on behalf of the artist. A reservation holds a piece for 48 hours.

          Classes and events

          Paid classes are refundable up to 72 hours before the start. Free events are first come, first served once seats are full.

          Privacy

          Last updated November 2026

          What we collect

          Your name, email or phone and order details when you order ahead, subscribe, book a seat or apply. A delivery address only if you choose local delivery or shipping.

          How we use it

          • To make and hand over your order.
          • To roast, pack and send your subscription.
          • To hold your seat at a class or show.
          • To reply to job and artist applications.

          What we never do

          We do not sell your information. Card payments are handled by our payment provider and never stored on this site.
