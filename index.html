            Terms

            Last updated November 2026

            Ordering ahead

            Orders are made for the pickup time you choose. If the case sells out after you order, we will offer a swap or a refund at the counter. Prices are before tax and may change; the order-ahead menu is always current.

            The Friday Takeaway

            Every Friday, every bag of coffee is 20% off in the shop and online. If a coffee is not on the shelf, order it online at the same price and pick it up once it is roasted.

            Subscriptions

            Subscriptions renew every two, four or six weeks. Skip, pause or cancel any time before the next roast day. The half-off first bag applies once per household while the first 100 spots last.

            Art, workshops and The Weekend Collection

            Work on the walls is sold on behalf of the artist. Private workshop dates are confirmed by email. Weekend Collection sign-ups are a waitlist, not an acceptance.

            Privacy

            Last updated November 2026

            What we collect

            Your name, email or phone and order details when you order ahead, subscribe, request a workshop, join a waitlist or apply. A delivery address only if you choose local delivery or shipping.

            How we use it

            • To make and hand over your order.
            • To roast, pack and send your subscription.
            • To plan your workshop or tell you when applications open.
            • To reply to job and wholesale enquiries.

            What we never do

            We do not sell your information. Card payments are handled by our payment provider and never stored on this site.
